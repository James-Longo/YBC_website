How to use it

Why Vortex?

Students and parents often ask what binoculars or scope to buy once they’re hooked. James always recommends Vortex, the brand he uses himself. The optics are solid, and the lifetime warranty is real: he got his binoculars replaced for free after years of heavy use wore them out.

Not sure what to get?

Binoculars and scopes are a big purchase. Ask James at any outing and he’ll help you pick. Or try the club’s loaner binoculars and scopes on outings first to see what you like.
